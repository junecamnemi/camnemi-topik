⭐ Taking TOPIK in Cambodia (Phnom Penh)

📍 Venue

Cambodia–Korea Cooperation Center (CKCC), Phnom Penh. Cambodia is a standing overseas test site — Korean and overseas sittings run on the same dates (e.g. 106th on May 17, 108th on Oct 18, 2026).

📝 How to register

  • Register in person at the Korean Embassy in Cambodia — not the Korean online portal
  • Opening hours: Mon–Sat 9:00–11:00 & 14:00–17:00
  • Windows are short (~1 week), ~7–8 weeks before the test date
  • Bring passport + photo + fee
Track the next Cambodia sitting. Follow the Korean Embassy in Cambodia (facebook.com/koreanembassycambodia) for the official notice, or let Camnemi remind you — tell us your target test date.

⭐ Also: SKA — the Sejong Korean test

Second certification path. The Sejong Korean Language Assessment (SKA / 세종한국어평가) is a government test now accepted by many Korean universities — including Korea University, Yonsei, Kookmin, Wonkwang, Yeungnam, and Anyang — plus scholarships (AMA+, POSCO TJ Park). For Cambodian students it's a realistic alternative or complement to TOPIK.
